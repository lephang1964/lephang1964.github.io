🚀 What Does This App Do?

ERPNext-Barcodes-Print is a simple yet powerful add-on for ERPNext that lets you print barcode labels straight to your label printer — no preview, no PDF, no extra steps. If you run a warehouse, shop, or factory that uses ERPNext, this tool saves you time every single day.

Imagine scanning a product or selecting an item in your ERPNext dashboard, then hitting "Print Label" — and your Zebra printer prints it immediately, exactly as it should. That's exactly what this app delivers.

💡 In plain words: you get lightning-fast barcode printing without the usual slow, complicated process.

✨ Why Choose ERPNext-Barcodes-Print?

  • ⚡ Direct-to-printer technology — communicates directly with your printer using QZ Tray or Zebra Browser Print. No PDFs, no temp files.
  • 🔤 Raw ZPL support — uses the printer's native language for fast, reliable printing with exact formatting.
  • 🖨️ Works with Zebra printers — ZD, ZT, and other ZPL-compatible models.
  • 📦 Built for ERPNext — integrates natively with Frappe Framework, no coding required.
  • 🛠️ Highly customizable — adjust label sizes, fields, barcode types, and fonts.

🖱️ How to Use (With ERPNext)

Using the app inside ERPNext is completely native. You'll see a new button on your item and product pages:

  • On any product page in ERPNext, look for the "Print Barcode" button (it appears naturally after installation).
  • Click the button, and a pop-up window shows you the label preview.
  • Click "Send to Printer" — the label prints instantly. Done!

💡 Pro tip: You can also add barcode buttons to your sales invoices, delivery notes, or warehouse bins by going to ERPNext Settings → Barcodes in the app.

🧠 How Does It Print So Fast?

Most barcode printing apps first create a PDF file, then send the PDF to the printer. That's slow — PDFs take time to generate, copy, and interpret.

This app skips ALL of that. Instead, it sends raw ZPL (the Zebra printer's native code) directly from ERPNext to your printer through a small helper program (QZ Tray or Zebra Browser Print). The printer understands that code immediately, so printing happens nearly instantly.

🔧 Example of how it feels: You click "Print Label" → your printer buzzes to life in under 1 second → label comes out perfectly formatted. No wait, no errors.

🎨 Make Labels Your Own

You don't need to be a designer or coder. The app includes a simple "Label Builder" with drag-and-drop controls:

  • Change label size (e.g., 1"x1", 2"x1", 4"x2")
  • Select barcode types (Code128, EAN, UPC, QR)
  • Add text, price, or product name to the label
  • Control font size, boldness, and alignment
  • Save multiple label templates for different products

The label builder is all visual — just click and type.
